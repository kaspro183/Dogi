How to buy DOGI

DOGI lives on Dogecoin as a DRC-20 inscription. It trades on Doggy Market, the marketplace for DOGI. You need a wallet that understands Doginals and a bit of DOGE.

Get a Doginals wallet

Install a wallet that supports DRC-20, like Spooky Doge or Doge Soft (see below). Back up your seed phrase offline.

Fund it with DOGE

Buy DOGE on any exchange and send it to your new wallet's address. Keep a little extra for network fees.

Buy on Doggy Market

Open DOGI on doggy.market, connect your wallet and pick a listing. Check the price per token before you confirm.

Hold or transfer

Your DOGI shows up in your wallet once confirmed. To send it, create a transfer inscription from a Doginals wallet.
