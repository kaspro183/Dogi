About DOGI

Dogecoin's original inscription.

Before DeFi, before L2s, there was DOGI. It brought fungible tokens to Dogecoin for the first time, inspired by Bitcoin's Ordinals and BRC-20, and it was minted entirely by the community.

21MHard-capped supply
0%Pre-mine or team reserve
Mar 112023 · deployed
May 112023 · fully minted
📜

What is DRC-20?

The fungible token standard of Dogecoin, built on Doginals. A small JSON payload is inscribed on-chain, and indexers compute balances from those inscriptions. No smart contracts needed.

⚖️

Fair from day one

No ICO, no pre-sale, no VC. Anyone could mint 1,000 DOGI per inscription until all 21 million were claimed two months after launch.

// The DOGI deploy inscription
{
  "p":    "drc-20",
  "op":   "deploy",
  "tick": "dogi",
  "max":  "21000000",
  "lim":  "1000"
}
🥇

Scarce by design

Dogecoin has an ongoing issuance. DOGI's supply is fixed forever at 21 million, the same cap as Bitcoin.

Ecosystem

DOGI × DogeOS × Laika

DogeOS is a zk rollup that brings EVM smart contracts to Dogecoin, built by the team behind the MyDoge wallet. Laika is building DeFi modules on top of it, and has proposed making DOGI one of the core tokens of the ecosystem.

🌉

Bridge

A DRC-20 ↔ DogeOS bridge so DOGI can move between Dogecoin and the app layer.

💧

DOGI/DOGE pool

Liquidity on BarkSwap, with part of the swap fees routed back to the DOGI treasury.

🔒

Staking

Single-asset DOGI staking through Laika's ve(3,3) engine (StakeOS).

🏛️

DOGI DAO

A community DAO to manage the treasury: liquidity, listings, marketing, incentives.

Guide

How to buy DOGI

DOGI lives on Dogecoin as a DRC-20 inscription. It trades on Doggy Market, the marketplace for DOGI. You need a wallet that understands Doginals and a bit of DOGE.

Get a Doginals wallet

Install a wallet that supports DRC-20, like Spooky Doge or Doge Soft (see below). Back up your seed phrase offline.

Fund it with DOGE

Buy DOGE on any exchange and send it to your new wallet's address. Keep a little extra for network fees.

Buy on Doggy Market

Open DOGI on doggy.market, connect your wallet and pick a listing. Check the price per token before you confirm.

Hold or transfer

Your DOGI shows up in your wallet once confirmed. To send it, create a transfer inscription from a Doginals wallet.
