DOGI DAO Watch

The DOGI DAO lets the community decide how the treasury is used. Voting runs on Laika's governance module on DogeOS. Right now everything happens on testnet: a dry run to learn how governance works before mainnet.

🧪 Testnet only. Test tokens have no real value. Never send real funds, and nobody official will ever DM you to "help you vote".

How to vote

Vote in four steps.

Your votes come from veDOGI: DOGI that you lock (stake) on Laika. You need a browser wallet (MetaMask works) connected to the DogeOS testnet.

Set up DogeOS

Add the testnet to MetaMask on the portal, then claim free test DOGE from the faucet.

Portal ↗ · Faucet ↗

Get test DOGI

Swap test DOGE for test DOGI on the BarkSwap pool.

BarkSwap pool ↗

Lock for veDOGI

Stake your DOGI for the lock length you choose. Longer locks give up to 3× voting power.

DOGI staking ↗

Vote

When a proposal is live, open the DOGI DAO on Laika and vote FOR or AGAINST.

DOGI DAO on Laika ↗

A proposal passes when more veDOGI votes FOR than AGAINST and at least 4% of all veDOGI has voted. Passed proposals are then executed on-chain from the treasury. These are the testnet rules and may change for mainnet. Telegram: type /dao in the group to see the proposals.

FAQ

About the DOGI DAO

🏛️

What is it?

A community treasury governed by votes. Proposals are simple yes/no questions, voted with veDOGI (locked DOGI).

💰

What funds the treasury?

On DogeOS, the treasury is planned to receive swap fees from the DOGI/DOGE pool and public donations. The test vote on a 1% bridge fee (proposal #7) passed.

🧪

Why testnet first?

To test the whole flow (proposals, votes, results) with tokens that have no value, before any real treasury exists on mainnet.
